Privacy policy

crypto-interface · last updated 20 November 2026

What the app does

crypto-interface records public market data from Polymarket and lets its users view and export it. It does not collect personal data for any other purpose.

Google account data

Connecting Google Drive is optional. The app asks only for the drive.file permission. With it the app can see and change only the files it created itself. It cannot read, list or change any other file on your Drive.

The app uses this permission for one thing: uploading the export files you ask for (CSV or SQLite in a ZIP) into its own folder on your Drive. It also reads your account's e-mail address to show which account is connected.

Storage and sharing

The access token Google issues is stored only on the server that runs the app and is used only to perform the uploads you ask for. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Google user data is not used to train any AI or machine-learning model.

Disconnecting

Disconnect in the app's Export dialog asks Google to revoke the token and deletes it from the server. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already uploaded stay on your Drive until you delete them.

Contact

Questions about this policy: the support e-mail shown on the Google sign-in screen of this app.
