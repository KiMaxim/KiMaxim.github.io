crypto-interface

A private tool for recording and viewing Polymarket crypto Up/Down markets.

crypto-interface records public market data from Polymarket (prices, order books, trades) and shows it as charts and tables. Its users can export the recorded data as CSV or SQLite files.

The Google Drive connection is optional. When a user connects their Google account, the app uploads only the export files that user asks for into a folder it creates on that user's Google Drive.
